# Verified facts

- Hearthstone applies enchantments in the order granted, auras after. Deaths are processed after the outermost phase ends, all at once. Source: hearthstone.wiki.gg Advanced rulebook, Set attribute page.
- MTG Arena's rules engine (GRE) is C++ plus CLIPS. Much of the card behavior code is machine-generated from card text. Source: magic.wizards.com "On Whiteboards, Naps, and Living Breakthrough".
- Forge card scripts separate abilities (`A:`), triggers (`T:`), statics (`S:`), and replacement effects (`R:`). Source: Card-Forge wiki, Card scripting API.
- Spellsource/Metastone cards are JSON `CardDesc` with `SpellDesc` trees and value providers. Source: playspellsource.com javadoc.
- YGO SEGOC order: turn player mandatory, non-turn player mandatory, turn player optional, non-turn player optional. Same category, the owner picks order. Source: Yugipedia "Simultaneous Effects", YGOrganization part 3.
- YGO spell speed: respond only with equal or higher speed. Spell Speed 1 can't respond. Source: Yugipedia "Spell Speed".
- YGO: optional "when... you can" triggers miss the timing unless their condition was the last thing to happen. "If" and mandatory triggers don't miss it. Source: Yugipedia "If... You Can VS When... You Can".
- OpenSpiel `State`: `legal_actions()`, `apply_action()` (in place), `child()` (clone + apply), `current_player()`, `is_terminal()`, `returns()`, `is_chance_node()`, `chance_outcomes()` giving `(action, prob)` pairs. `kChancePlayerId = -1`, simultaneous `-2`. Chance moves are ordinary actions in explicit-stochastic mode. In sampled mode the game keeps its own RNG. Source: open_spiel docs/concepts.md, spiel.h, spiel_globals.h.
- SabberStone: `Controller.Options()` returns `List<PlayerTask>`, empty for the player who isn't acting. `Game.Process(task)` mutates in place. `Game.Clone(...)` for search. Source: SabberStoneCore Controller.cs, Game.cs.
- Metastone: public `GameContext.getValidActions()`. `performAction` is private; `GameLogic.performGameAction(playerId, action)` is public. The engine calls out with `IBehaviour.requestAction(context, player, validActions)`. Source: demilich1/metastone GameContext.java, IBehaviour.java.
- Forge: the engine calls out to `PlayerController` (`chooseSpellAbilityToPlay()`, `declareAttackers(...)`), with human and AI implementations. Source: Card-Forge PlayerController.java, PhaseHandler.java.
- Rust std `HashMap`: each instance gets its own random seed, so iteration order differs even within one process. Source: doc.rust-lang.org HashMap, RandomState.
- MTG CR 400.7: "An object that moves from one zone to another becomes a new object with no memory of, or relation to, its previous existence." Exceptions 400.7a onward. 704.3: SBAs are checked whenever a player would get priority, all applicable ones are performed simultaneously as one event, and the check repeats. 104.4a: "If all the players remaining in a game lose simultaneously, the game is a draw." Layer 7 sublayers are 613.4a to 613.4d: CDA, set, modify (counters merged into 7c in the Ikoria update, 2020), switch. Timestamp order within a sublayer (613.7). Source: yawgatog CR (effective 2026-09-25).
- MTG Arena GRE sends `AnnotationType_ObjectIdChanged` (from `orig_id` to `new_id`) when a zone move gives a card a new instance ID. Source: manasight-parser (third-party; the protocol is undocumented).
- Hearthstone: both heroes dead at the end of a sequence is a draw (Hellfire example). Arcane Giant: "Costs (1) less for each spell you've cast this game." Moving "backwards" (Play to Hand) resets tags and enchantments (Z5a), while hand buffs survive being played. Health rule H2: when max Health drops, current Health drops only if it exceeds the new max, so a damaged minion survives losing an aura (MTG: it dies). Deaths are processed only after the outermost phase: Aura Update, then Death Creation Step (simultaneous), then Death Phases, repeated until no new deaths. Source: hearthstone.wiki.gg Advanced rulebook, Health, Return to hand.
- Forge: `GameAction.checkStaticAbilities()` calls `StaticEffects.clearStaticEffects` (removes every applied static effect) and then reapplies all continuous statics layer by layer (timestamp order, dependencies per 613.8). It runs at the start of every pass of `checkStateEffects`'s SBA loop (`for q < 9`, a capped fixed point) and also inside `changeZone`. Source: Card-Forge GameAction.java, StaticEffects.java.
- Hearthstone Aura Updates: after the outermost Phase there's an Aura Update (Health/Attack), then the Death Creation Step, then an Aura Update (Other). Both also run whenever a minion is summoned (not played). Auras aren't recalculated mid-Phase, so stats can be stale (the Mana Wyrm and Cone of Cold example). Source: hearthstone.wiki.gg Advanced rulebook, 4a and 4b.
- YGO: a monster flipped face-down or leaving the field loses effects previously applied to it (Relay Soul and Junk Synchron rulings). The "treated as a new card" wording is community phrasing. Source: Yugipedia card rulings.
- Session 03 engine reports, full text in `docs/course/research/03-events/`. Forge: rules read "this turn" fields (`lifeLostThisTurn`, `MagicStack.thisTurnCast`, `leftBattlefieldThisTurn`) reset at cleanup; triggers go through `TriggerHandler.runTrigger` into a waiting list put on the stack when a player would get priority, and `collectTriggerForWaiting()` snapshots matching triggers at event time; UI gets `GameEvent`s on a Guava `EventBus` ("sent to UI, log and sound system") plus `TrackableObject` views, with `CardView.canBeShownTo(viewer)` deciding visibility; the AI simulates on a `GameCopier` copy with no GUI subscribers. XMage: Watchers see every `GameEvent` before triggers (`GameState.handleEvent`) and reset at end of turn; replacement effects see the event first (`replaceEvent` then mutate then `fireEvent`); the client gets full per-player `GameView` snapshots plus log text, no animation events; old replays stored `GameState` copies ("outdated and not used"); AI copies set a `simulation` flag that skips notifications. SabberStone: tag counters plus `PlayHistory`; triggers are C# delegates queuing tasks; `PowerHistory` off by default in `Clone()`. Hearthstone protocol: `TAG_CHANGE` diffs grouped in nested `BLOCK_START/END` (PLAY, ATTACK, TRIGGER, DEATHS, FATIGUE, ...), `META_DATA` guides animations, hidden cards have no card ID until `SHOW_ENTITY`, a per-player dispatcher holds back packets (HearthSim). HSReplay is XML of that stream. Metastone/Spellsource: one `GameEvent` feeds triggers and UI; Spellsource sends each event with a redacted snapshot (`ModelConversions`, "Censor the opponent hand and deck entities") and stores replays as a `Trace` of seed, decks, mulligans and action indices, re-simulated to view. Arena GRE: `Full` then `Diff` `GameStateMessage`s with `annotations` (`DamageDealt`, `ZoneTransfer` with a category, `ObjectIdChanged`), zones with `Visibility` and `viewers`, hidden cards sent as IDs with no object; draws and shuffles reissue IDs (reason undocumented).
- Bevy 0.17 split buffered events into messages (`Message`, `MessageWriter`, `MessageReader`, `Messages<M>`), and `Event` now means observer events (`commands.trigger`). Latest stable 0.19.1, 0.20.0-rc.2 out (crates.io, 2026-09-28). Source: bevy.org 0.17 release notes and migration guide.
- Fowler, Event Sourcing: "Capture all changes to an application state as a sequence of events." Command sourcing (store the inputs, rerun the decisions) is a named pattern in Akka's persistence docs. Source: martinfowler.com/eaaDev/EventSourcing.html, doc.akka.io.
- Session 04 targeting and effect data, full text in `docs/course/research/04-effects/targeting-and-effect-data.md`. Hearthstone: "Players cannot take an action and choose "no target" if it normally requires one", while "minions are never prevented from being played due to a lack of Battlecry targets" (hearthstone.wiki.gg Target). Old HearthstoneJSON builds carry `playRequirements`: Execute `REQ_TARGET_TO_PLAY` plus minion, enemy and damaged filters; Fire Elemental `REQ_TARGET_IF_AVAILABLE`; Elven Archer `REQ_NONSELF_TARGET` (the current feed has no such field). SabberStone: `PlayCardTask(controller, source, target, zonePosition, chooseOne)`, `Controller.Options()` adds one task per target and skips a `MustHaveTargetToPlay` card with none. Metastone: card-level `targetSelection`, `ActionLogic.rollout` clones the action per valid target; `MetaSpell` runs its children in order; Hellfire is one `DamageSpell` on `ALL_CHARACTERS`. Forge: `ValidTgts$` (targeted) vs `Defined$` ("Remember this is non-targeted!"), `SubAbility$` chains resolve after the parent (Electrolyze, Chandra's Outrage with `Defined$ TargetedController`).
- MTG CR (yawgatog, effective 2026-09-25): 601.2c targets are announced while casting, costs later (601.2h), and a step that can't be completed makes the cast illegal and "the game returns to the moment before the casting of that spell was proposed" (601.2). 608.2b: a spell with all targets illegal doesn't resolve; otherwise illegal targets are skipped and the rest still happens. 608.2c: instructions in the order written. 608.2d: other choices are made "while applying the effect". 704.4: "state-based actions pay no attention to what happens during the resolution of a spell or ability." 118.12 "if you do" checks whether the player paid or chose, "regardless of what events actually occurred". "Then" has no CR definition. 109.1 lists objects (players aren't one); 120.3a damage to a player is life loss, 120.3e damage to a creature is marked.
- YGO conjunctions (YGOrganization, Demystifying Rulings Part 5): simultaneous + B needs A = "and if you do"; sequential + B needs A = "then"; simultaneous + independent = "also"; sequential + independent = "also, after that". Plain "and": both must be possible or neither happens. A never needs B. "Then" makes a "When ... you can" trigger on A miss the timing.
- Hearthstone entities (HearthSim protocol docs): one `Game` (ID 1), two `Player`s (IDs 2 and 3), and everything else is a `Card`, the hero included (`CARDTYPE` `HERO`, linked by the player's `HERO_ENTITY` tag). Hero cards and Jaraxxus replace the hero, and modifiers on the old hero don't carry over. SabberStone: `Hero : Character`, `Minion : Character`, `Controller` separate. Tokens are uncollectible cards.
- Rust fn pointers implement `Clone`, `Copy`, `PartialEq`, `Eq`, `Hash`, `Debug` for every signature, higher-ranked ones included (built-in `FnPtr`, 1.70). Comparing them is unreliable, and `unpredictable_function_pointer_comparisons` (1.85) fires inside `#[derive(PartialEq)]` since 1.89. On rustc 1.92 here, `Debug` prints an address. `Box<dyn Fn>` derives none of `Debug`, `Clone`, `PartialEq`. Source: doc.rust-lang.org/std/primitive.fn.html, rust-lang/rust#134536, and a check on rustc 1.92 in session 04.
- Session 04c tokens, summon and player targets, full text in `docs/course/research/04-effects/tokens-summon-and-player-targets.md`. MTG (yawgatog, effective 2026-09-25): a token represents "any permanent that isn't represented by a card" (111.1); in another zone it ceases to exist as a state-based action (111.7, 704.5d), after its zone-change triggers fire; a token that left the battlefield can't move again (111.8). "Any target" is 115.4: creatures, players, planeswalkers or battles. Damage to a player normally means life loss (119.2, 120.3a; infect gives poison, 120.3b). Hearthstone (hearthstone.wiki.gg): Battlecry fires only when played from the hand, not when summoned by another card (Shudderwock-style repeats aside); every minion is "summoned", only one played from the hand is "played" (Reinforce's Recruit triggers Sword of Justice, not Mirror Entity); a bounced uncollectible card goes to the hand and plays like any other card; Resurrect-style effects read the Death Event Cache, not the graveyard; heroes have 30 base Health in Constructed, no Attack by default, and "minion" effects can't target them, only "character" ones; a hero card keeps damage, Health and Armor and drops enchantments, while Jaraxxus sets Health to 15. Whether a replaced hero gets a new entity ID is unverified. Yu-Gi-Oh (Rulebook v10): 8000 LP; Tokens are not in the Deck and can't go anywhere but the field, so destroyed or returned they are "simply removed from the field"; always face-up.
- Session 04a random targets and AoE order, full text in `docs/course/research/04-effects/random-targets-and-aoe-order.md`. Hearthstone: a random effect with no valid target does nothing (Bomb Lobber's note, Knife Juggler in the Advanced rulebook), and random damage skips minions already at 0 health; Deadly Shot carries `REQ_MINIMUM_ENEMY_MINIONS: 1` in HearthstoneJSON build 25770, so it can't be played into an empty enemy board, while Mad Bomber and Arcane Missiles have no requirement. AoE (Advanced rulebook DH1): all damage events are created in play order (heroes are older than every minion), then resolved in play order; DH3 lists exceptions that resolve each hit as it's created (Lightning Storm, Lightbomb). Metastone: targets are flat group constants (`EntityReference`: `ENEMY_MINIONS`, `ALL_CHARACTERS`, `FRIENDLY_HERO`, `SELF`, ...), "a random X" is a group plus `"randomTarget": true`, an empty list casts nothing, and Deadly Shot's gate is a card `condition`. SabberStone: `RandomTask(1, EntityType.OP_MINIONS)` then a task on `EntityType.STACK`; on an empty set `RandomTask` returns `STOP`, which skips the rest of the chain.
- Session 04c player vs card targets in MTG engines, full text in `docs/course/research/04-effects/forge-xmage-player-targets.md`. Forge: `Player` and `Card` both extend the abstract `GameEntity` (implements the `GameObject` interface), which holds id, counters and an abstract `addDamageAfterPrevention`; `Player` keeps `int life`, `Card` keeps marked damage; `TargetChoices` is a list of `GameObject` (players, cards, spell abilities); player and card ids come from separate counters, and `equals` compares the class too. XMage: `Player` and `MageObject` share only `MageItem` (`UUID getId()`); targets are `List<UUID>`; players and objects get random UUIDs; `TargetPermanentOrPlayer` and `DamageTargetEffect` look each id up as a permanent, then as a player, and call that side's own `damage`. Source: Card-Forge/forge and magefree/mage `master`.
- Session 04b multi-target drafts, full text in `docs/course/research/04-effects/multi-target-drafts.md`. MTG CR (yawgatog, effective 2026-09-25) 115.3 and 601.2c: one instance of "target" can't pick the same object twice ("Tap two target creatures" needs two different creatures), but separate instances can each pick it ("Destroy target artifact and target land" can target one artifact land twice). No rule defines "another target"; it is card text (Leeching Bite, Arc Trail). 601.2 plus 733.1: a cast step that can't be completed reverses the whole cast and cancels payments. Forge: "two target" is one ability with `TargetMin$ 2 | TargetMax$ 2` (Undo); "another target" is a SubAbility with its own `ValidTgts$` and `TargetUnique$ True`, enforced by excluding the parents' targets (Leeching Bite). XMage: several `addTarget` calls on one ability (Decimate), `TargetCreaturePermanent(2)` sets min and max to 2 (Undo), "another" is `setTargetTag` plus `AnotherTargetPredicate` (Leeching Bite). Hearthstone: "Players can only choose one target per action" (wiki Target page), but Barbed Nets picks a second target with a Naga condition, and its notes say the first target can't be undone or cancelled while assigning the second and the same target can't be chosen twice. MTG Arena's deselect-on-click is unconfirmed.
- Session 05 MTG triggers, replacements and last known information, full text in `docs/course/research/05-triggers/mtg-cr.md` (CR effective 2026-09-25). 603.2: a trigger "automatically triggers" when its event occurs and "doesn't do anything at this point"; 603.2g: a prevented or replaced event triggers nothing. 603.3: it goes on the stack "the next time a player would receive priority"; 603.3b: APNAP, each player orders their own (now a two-pass process). 603.4: an intervening "if" is checked at the event and again on resolution. 603.10: normally the state just after the event decides; 603.10a leaves-the-battlefield abilities "look back in time", so a "whenever a creature dies" artifact destroyed with two creatures triggers twice. 608.2h and 113.7a: an effect whose object left its zone uses last known information; an ability on the stack is independent of its source. 614.1 "instead" is a replacement; 614.5 a replacement "gets only one opportunity to affect an event"; 614.6 a replaced event never happens. 616.1: the affected object's controller or the affected player picks the order, a to f, repeating on the modified event. 615: prevention is a sibling of replacement. 604.1 and 611.3a: statics are "simply true" and not "locked in". 704.3 and 117.5: SBAs, then waiting triggers onto the stack, repeated until neither happens, then priority.
- Session 05 Hearthstone timing, full text in `docs/course/research/05-triggers/hearthstone-timing.md` (hearthstone.wiki.gg Advanced rulebook, examples mostly 2015 to 2017). Triggers queue "in order of play" (the order their entities entered play, oldest first), a queue is immutable once it starts, and resolution is depth-first. After the outermost Phase: Aura Update, then the Death Creation Step removes all mortally wounded entities at once, and "Entities that have been removed from play cannot trigger, be triggered, or emit auras"; then a Death Phase resolves each death's triggers in order of play, repeating. Cult Master and Flesheating Ghoul don't trigger on minions dying at the same time. Mortally wounded minions stay in play and keep triggering (Acolyte of Pain, Armorsmith). "Whenever" cast triggers (Questing Adventurer, Mana Wyrm, Violet Teacher) resolve in the On Play Phase, before the Spell Text Phase; "after" triggers in the After Spell Phase. Divine Shield acts while the Damage Event is created (DH2a) and a damage event that changes nothing runs no triggers (DH2b); Predamage triggers (Bolf Ramshield, Ice Block) prevent and replace the event (DH7). Silence removes deathrattles and triggers. Undocumented: silence while mortally wounded, which Attack a deathrattle reads, a cap on trigger chains (only an 89-turn limit).
- Session 05 engine trigger sources, full text in `docs/course/research/05-triggers/forge-xmage-triggers.md` and `sabberstone-metastone-triggers.md`. Forge: a cached `activeTriggers` list, patched on zone change and rebuilt from every card's current traits (`resetActiveTriggers`) after the SBA loop; look-back triggers registered from LKI copies of the last battlefield state (`registerActiveLTBTrigger`); replacements run before the move (`ReplacementHandler.run`, the affected player chooses, `setHasRun` applies each once). XMage: a registry (`GameState.triggers`) filled when a card loads and not pruned on ability loss (`removeAllAbilities` carries a TODO about triggers); `isInUseableZone`/`hasSourceObjectAbility` re-check at each event, against LKI for dies triggers; `checkTriggered` walks APNAP and each player picks the order; `replaceEvent` before the mutation, `getAppliedEffects` for apply-once. SabberStone: triggers subscribe C# delegates on entering the board and unsubscribe on leaving or silence; tasks queue per event and drain in `ProcessTasks`; deaths sorted by `OrderOfPlay`, deathrattles read from the dead minion's card and queued; enchantments stripped in the graveyard before the deathrattle runs. Metastone: one flat listener list scanned on every event, triggers resolved inline; deaths sorted by entity id, deathrattles read from the dead actor; a death-loop cap of 20 passes.
- Session 05 Legends of Runeterra timing, full text in `docs/course/research/05-triggers/lor-timing.md` (community wiki and player tests; Riot publishes no comprehensive rules). Board triggers on deaths "only activate if they survive the effect" (Neverglade Collector; Tortured Prodigy is an exception); Last Breath "triggers after the effect has resolved"; triggers fire sequentially, deck then hand then board left to right, allies first; skills go on the spell stack and resolve last in first out; units at 0 stay targetable until the spell finishes; deaths are processed between sequential Round Start triggers ("the Ravine ends up killing the Maiden and Slotbot before their effects resolve").
- Session 05 Yu-Gi-Oh! timing, full text in `docs/course/research/05-triggers/ygo-timing.md`. Since the April 2020 Master Rule revision, an effect that activates on the field can't activate if its card left the field before activation (official OCG Q&A on Heavymetalfoes Electrumite destroyed by Icarus Attack); GY-activating "sent to the GY" effects still activate after simultaneous destruction (Dark Hole examples); effect destruction happens as the chain link resolves, battle destruction reaches the GY at the end of the Damage Step; trigger effects wait for the whole chain, then form a new chain in SEGOC order; no general state check, only continuous self-destroy effects applied immediately.
- Session 05 Lorcana, Flesh and Blood and KeyForge timing, full text in `docs/course/research/05-triggers/lorcana-fab-keyforge-timing.md`. Lorcana CR 7.4.3: cards leaving play together "see" each other leave (Lyle Tiberius Rourke triggers twice); 6.7.6 last known value; 1.8.1 and 6.7.4: a game state check "after any effect resolves", including each triggered effect from the bag (7.7.4.3). Flesh and Blood: 5.4.6a a source that ceases to exist from the triggering event still triggers; 1.2.3 last known information; 1.10.2 game state actions on every new priority state, so after each layer resolves (5.3.7); "another ally dies" in a multi-event unsettled. KeyForge v18: an "after a card is destroyed" ability's card "must be in play at the time the destroyed card leaves play"; "Destroyed:" abilities resolve while the card is still in play, then all destroyed cards go to discard together; no state check, destruction is immediate; the active player orders simultaneous effects.
- Session 05 Marvel Snap, Shadowverse and Gwent timing, full text in `docs/course/research/05-triggers/snap-shadowverse-gwent-timing.md` (Snap from Second Dinner developer answers; Shadowverse and Gwent community-sourced). Snap: a destroy effect locks its targets, destroys them one at a time in play order, and each "when destroyed" resolves before the next. Shadowverse: a follower destroyed in a sweep doesn't trigger its "whenever another follower is destroyed"; Last Words resolve turn player first, earliest summoned first, without interruption. Gwent: Deathwish fires on moving from board to graveyard; simultaneous effects resolve melee before ranged, left to right, turn player first (disputed for Deathwish).
